## Definition

A normative counter-position to the diagnosis that stupidity is humanity's central problem and general intelligence its solution. It holds that there are two distinct modes of knowing — one narrow and instrumental, which categorizes and manipulates; one holistic and hermeneutic, which attends and relates — that the second has atrophied relative to the first, and that scaling the first without the second yields more capability directed at unexamined ends.

The claim is about *direction* rather than *capacity*: if one does not know which port to sail for, no wind is favorable.

## Intuition

The contrast is between having more ability and knowing what to do with it. Someone can be described as "too smart for" a role — brilliant, and consequently ineffective at the thing that mattered — while a less clever colleague transforms a difficult situation through sustained attention to people.

The mechanism proposed is that attention is itself a mode of knowing. In Iris Murdoch's formulation, love is the quality of the attention we pay; listening well is on this account an act of love, and several contemplative traditions independently converge on "listen with your heart's ear" — a phrasing that presupposes *intending* to hear the other rather than waiting for one's turn to speak. Knowledge acquired this way is not a softer version of analytical knowledge; it is knowledge of things analytical knowledge does not reach.

Underneath sits an anthropological fork. Either humans are atomistic units with negligible ties to natural and social habitats that exist primarily for use and extraction — in which case care and goodwill are decorative — or humans are constitutively interdependent, in which case those capacities are central to flourishing and their neglect is a first-order civilizational problem rather than a matter of taste.

## Variants

- **Domain-limited reading** — instrumental intelligence is the binding constraint in some domains (mathematics, theoretical science) and not in others (governance, conflict, care). This is the version most compatible with evidence, and the one that survives the observation that scaling models does appear to deliver in symbolic domains.
- **Civilizational reading** — the strong claim, that a civilization with meagre capacity to see and cherish its members becomes *less* competent and wise as it becomes more narrowly intelligent and powerful.
- **Objective-specification reading** — the technically tractable version: a system optimizing an instrumental objective will classify care-driven and generous behavior as error rather than as signal.
- **Practice reading** — wisdom as a muscle maintained by specific practices (structured encounter across difference, deep listening) rather than a property to be designed in.

## Comparison

- **Against [[marginal-returns-to-intelligence]]**: the same conclusion — adding intelligence to a system whose binding constraint lies elsewhere buys little — reached normatively rather than analytically. Marginal returns asks empirically which complementary factors bind; this asks whether the ends being served are the right ones. The two are complementary, and marginal returns is the more testable of the pair.
- **Against [[universal-ai-intelligence-measure]]**: directly denies that a single scalar capacity measure captures what matters, since the second mode of knowing is by construction not a quantity of processing.
- **Against [[cognitive-surrender]]**: adjacent but distinct. Cognitive surrender concerns individuals ceding judgment to AI output; this concerns a civilization cultivating one mode of knowing at the expense of another, independent of whether any individual defers to a machine.
- **Toward [[pluralistic-alignment]] and [[positive-alignment]]**: supplies a reason those research programmes matter — if the objective does not encode relational goods, a competent optimizer will treat them as noise.

## Known limitations

- **The central terms are undefined.** "Wisdom," and "love as the quality of attention," are evocative but not operationalized, so the comparative claim cannot be assessed or tested.
- The supporting case is authority stacking: poets, philosophers, a novelist and a papal encyclical selected for agreement. Convergence among chosen authorities is not evidence.
- The two-modes framing rests on a contested neuroscientific account (McGilchrist) whose empirical standing is disputed.
- Counter-examples are self-selected civic success stories reported without outcome measurement.
- Silent on the obvious objection that AI systems might be *better* at sustained, undistracted attention than humans are — which would invert the argument rather than answer it.
- Persuasion is conceded to work somatically, through relationship rather than argument, which limits the concept's reach as an argumentative position.

## Open problems

- Can quality of attention be specified precisely enough to be designed for or measured? Without this the position remains a mood rather than a research direction.
- **The tractable test**: do current systems in fact classify generous and care-driven behavior as error, in the way much of economics long treated generous play in ultimatum games as anomaly? This is directly checkable and connects the concept to alignment work.
- Is the instrumental/hermeneutic split a genuine constraint on machine cognition or an artifact of a contested framing?
- If wisdom is constituted by relationship and interdependence rather than information processing, is it in principle unavailable to a system that has neither — and what would count as evidence either way?
- What would a system optimized for the second mode of knowing look like, concretely?

## Relationship to foundations

Draws on a virtue-ethics distinction between *phronesis* (practical wisdom) and technical skill, and on the humanistic tradition treating attention as constitutive of moral perception (Murdoch, Weil). The framing of two modes of knowing is taken from Iain McGilchrist. The economic reference point is the treatment of generous play in ultimatum-game experiments as deviation from rationality — a documented instance of an instrumental framework classifying prosocial behavior as error.

## My understanding

One part of this is worth taking seriously as more than sentiment: the objective-specification reading. A system optimizing an instrumental objective will read costly care and generosity as error, exactly as a generation of economists read them as anomalies in the ultimatum game. That is a concrete failure mode, it is checkable, and it states the case for pluralistic and prosocial alignment objectives more cleanly than most technical treatments do.

The civilizational version is much weaker, and its weakness is specific: it cannot be evaluated because neither "wisdom" nor "love as attention" is pinned down, and the case for it is made by assembling authorities who agree. The domain-limited reading is the defensible one. Mathematics appears to be a domain where instrumental intelligence genuinely is the binding constraint and scaling it delivers — which is strong evidence that the argument is domain-specific rather than general, and the concept is more useful if stated that way.
