## Recent work

Co-founder and Chief AGI Scientist at Google DeepMind. Senior author on [[from-agi-to-asi]] (2026), which uses the Legg-Hutter / Universal AI framework he helped originate to ground a continuum from human-level AGI to artificial general superintelligence and map the technological pathways and frictions between them.
