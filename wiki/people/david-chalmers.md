## Research areas

- Philosophy of mind, especially the metaphysics and epistemology of consciousness (the "hard problem")
- Extended and distributed cognition
- Philosophical implications of artificial intelligence and virtual reality

## Recent work

- [[extended-mind]] — with Andy Clark, argues via the parity principle and the Otto/Inga thought experiment that cognitive processes and dispositional beliefs can be partly constituted by reliably-coupled external resources, founding the extended-cognition research program.

## My notes

Best known outside this wiki for framing the "hard problem of consciousness"; this 1998 paper (co-authored with Andy Clark, who is credited by the authors' own footnote as holding somewhat less belief in the thesis than Chalmers) predates the AI-specific literature this wiki otherwise tracks by decades but supplies much of its underlying conceptual vocabulary for thinking about human-AI cognitive coupling.
