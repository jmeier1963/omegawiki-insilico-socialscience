## Research areas

- Virtual and agentic economies for AI systems
- Market design and institutional infrastructure for autonomous scientific discovery
- AI governance implications of agent-native economic systems

## Recent work

- [[agentic-economies-autonomous-scientific-discovery]] — corresponding author (with Franklin, Kasirzadeh, Natarajan, Karthikesalingam, Krier, and Osindero), proposing the infrastructure of a native Automated Scientific Economy — markets, idea exchanges, agent-native artefacts, credit/IP frameworks, and biosecurity safeguards — needed for AI scientists and humans to jointly allocate scarce scientific resources.
