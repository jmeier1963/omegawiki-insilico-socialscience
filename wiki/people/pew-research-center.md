## Research areas

- Nonpartisan public opinion polling and demographic research
- Survey research methodology and data science
- Evaluating emerging polling methods, including AI-generated synthetic samples

## Recent work

- [[ai-stand-human-survey-takers-really]] — Chapekis, Lau, Bestvater, Shah, Mercer & Smith (2026): large-scale "digital twin" replication of three American Trends Panel waves finding AI-generated synthetic survey responses differ from matched human responses by an average of 12 percentage points, with the Center concluding it has "no current or future plans to use AI models to generate survey results."

## My notes

A long-established, nonpartisan, nonadvocacy public-opinion research institution (not an academic lab or AI company), giving this report's negative findings on AI-generated synthetic surveys unusual institutional weight and credibility within the ongoing silicon-sampling methodology debate, backed by the Center's own decades of probability-sample survey infrastructure as the ground-truth comparison.
