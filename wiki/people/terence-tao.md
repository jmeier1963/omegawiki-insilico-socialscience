## Research areas

- Pure mathematics across analysis, PDE, number theory, and combinatorics
- Formal verification and AI-assisted mathematics (Lean, large-scale collaborative proof projects)
- Commentary on how AI is reshaping mathematical research culture and incentives

## Recent work

Gave the invited talk [[math-terence-tao-caltech-talk-proof]] ("Math 2.0", Caltech, October 2026), arguing that AI's objective verifiability, digitizability, and reliance on large datasets are driving mathematics from an era of proof scarcity into an era of proof abundance, and that the field must expand its research frontier and decenter problem-solving as a proxy for progress. Cited large-scale collaborative projects including the Equational Theories Project and the Inverse Galois Problem challenge (IGP24) as prototypes of this shift.

## My notes

A leading figure whose public commentary carries outsized weight in how the broader mathematical community interprets AI's impact on the field; his "lighthouse" framing of open problems (as exploration-guiding landmarks rather than mere targets) is a notable conceptual contribution for thinking about premature AI-driven problem-solving.
