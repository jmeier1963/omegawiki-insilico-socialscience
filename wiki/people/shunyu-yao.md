## Key papers

- [[cognitive-architectures-language-agents]] — co-first author (equal contribution with Sumers); proposed CoALA, a unified cognitive architecture framework for language agents (TMLR 2024)

## Recent work

Co-first author on CoALA (2023). Also known for ReAct (reasoning + acting interleaved) and Tree of Thoughts. Associated with Karthik Narasimhan's lab at Princeton; later joined OpenAI.
