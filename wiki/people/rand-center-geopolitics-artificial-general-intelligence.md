## Research areas

- National-security and geopolitical implications of AGI/superintelligence
- Comparative analysis of state-level AI strategy options
- Feasibility and effectiveness of prospective AGI-enabled capabilities

## Recent work

- [[strategy-secure-geopolitical-advantage-uncertain-path]] — a 12-author team publication (Predd, Boudreaux, Chessen, Cibralic, Geist, Horton, Kerrigan, Marcellino, Mei, Mondschein, Moon, Sytsma) proposing a "Freedom of Action" U.S. AI strategy built around a seven-archetype taxonomy and five pivotal uncertainties, following on the Center's companion paper "Finding Common Ground in AGI Strategy Debates" (Predd et al., 2026).

## My notes

A RAND-internal center explicitly organized around convening technologists, strategists, economists, and political scientists on AGI geopolitics — distinct from RAND's general AI work in treating superintelligence-era strategy as its dedicated mandate. The 12-author byline reflects a genuinely collective research product rather than a single-PI paper, which is why this entity is tracked as an organization rather than under an individual researcher's page.
