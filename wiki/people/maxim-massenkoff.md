## Research areas

- Causal effects of AI adoption on employment and productivity
- Labor-market restructuring under agentic AI
- Enterprise-level economic research on AI diffusion

## Recent work

- [[how-employment-changing-anthropic]] — with Nathan Wilmers, Regina Catipon, and Peter McCrory, uses comprehensive internal Anthropic data and a staggered-adoption natural experiment to document AI's causal effects on productivity, hiring composition, and work organization within the company.

## My notes

Prior work by Massenkoff and McCrory on AI employment outcomes was already cited in this wiki's [[ai-adoption-depth-breadth-gap]] concept as a contrast case to exposure-based measures, before this paper (their fuller internal Anthropic study) was directly ingested — suggesting a sustained, specific research focus on measuring AI's actual employment effects rather than its theoretical exposure.
