## Research areas

- Universal Artificial Intelligence (AIXI) and the theoretical foundations of machine intelligence
- Algorithmic information theory and Solomonoff induction
- Co-originator of the Legg-Hutter intelligence measure

## Recent work

Senior researcher at Google DeepMind and professor at the Australian National University. Co-author on [[from-agi-to-asi]] (2026); the report's formal grounding rests on his AIXI / Universal AI framework (Hutter 2005; Hutter et al. 2024) and the Legg-Hutter intelligence score.

## My notes

The principal theorist behind the [[universal-ai-intelligence-measure]] concept: AIXI provides the strongest known theoretical upper bound for machine intelligence, used in this report as the incomputable endpoint that practical ASIs approximate from below.
