## Research areas

- LLM-based social simulation and agent-based modeling at scale
- Automated, natural-language-driven simulation construction
- Autonomous research-agent systems for social science

## Recent work

- [[yulan-onesim-towards-next-generation-social]] — led by Lei Wang, Heyang Gao, Xiaohe Bo, Xu Chen, and Ji-Rong Wen, introduces a code-free, feedback-evolving, 100,000-agent-scale social simulator with an autonomous "AI social researcher" layer, fully open-sourced.
