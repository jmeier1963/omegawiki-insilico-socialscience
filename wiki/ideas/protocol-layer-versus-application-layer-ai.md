## Hypothesis

Protocol-layer AI regulation (compute, chips, APIs, model weights) is more resilient to Goodhart's Law gaming than application-layer content regulation, analogous to network neutrality vs. content moderation. The TCP/IP analogy implies that robust AI governance requires minimal, stable protocol-layer rules.

## Approach sketch

Eliminated — core framing already established in Constitutional Stack (2025-2026), Three-Layer Global AI Governance model, and IETF AIIP drafts.

## Lessons learned

TCP/IP analogy for AI governance is already established discourse. Future work should differentiate on the specific Goodhart-resistance property rather than the protocol/application distinction.
