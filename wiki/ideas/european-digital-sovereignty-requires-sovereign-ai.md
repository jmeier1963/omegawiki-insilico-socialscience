## Statement

Europe's heavy dependence on US- and China-based AI infrastructure and models constitutes a strategic vulnerability. Achieving genuine digital sovereignty requires building indigenous AI platforms, cloud infrastructure, and federated data spaces — not only regulatory frameworks.

## Evidence summary

EuroStack report (2025) and EU digital policy analysis document this dependence and propose sovereign infrastructure solutions. Confidence low-moderate: the claim is largely normative; empirical evidence for effectiveness of sovereign AI strategies is limited.

## Counter-evidence

- Pure regulatory sovereignty (GDPR, AI Act) may achieve policy goals without physical infrastructure investment
- Sovereign AI platforms risk significant competitiveness gaps vs US/China frontier models
- High cost of sovereign infrastructure may crowd out other EU innovation investment

## Linked ideas

## Open questions

- Is regulatory sovereignty (AI Act) sufficient, or is infrastructure sovereignty (EuroStack) necessary?
- Can EU sovereign AI attract top global talent at competitive terms?
