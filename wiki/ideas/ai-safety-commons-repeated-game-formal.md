## Hypothesis

The AI safety commons problem is a multi-player repeated prisoner's dilemma; compute governance, mandatory disclosure, and joint audit can sustain cooperation equilibria.

## Approach sketch

Eliminated — RAND RRA4245-1 and arXiv:2501.15280 already publish formal models of this class.

## Lessons learned

AI race as prisoner's dilemma is well-covered. Incremental differentiation on the compute+disclosure+audit mechanism triplet or heterogeneous-actor multi-player extension is insufficient for standalone contribution. If pursuing this direction, synthesize Cooperative AI Foundation work + RAND RRA4245-1 to identify specific uninvestigated mechanisms.
