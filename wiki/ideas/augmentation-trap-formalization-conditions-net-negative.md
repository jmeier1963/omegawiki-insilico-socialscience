## Hypothesis

AI augmentation produces net-negative human capital effects when: (1) performance enhancement is immediate and visible; (2) the augmented skill is the same as the skill being learned; (3) there is no deliberate scaffolding-removal mechanism.

## Lessons learned

Eliminated — core mechanism covered by active ideas in science context. Labor market angle needs stronger differentiation via labor economics framing rather than cognitive/epistemic framing.
