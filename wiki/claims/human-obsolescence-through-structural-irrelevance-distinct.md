## Statement

The three dominant AI risk categories (misalignment, loss of control, catastrophe) all presuppose AI doing something to humans through conflict or error. A structurally distinct fourth risk — "extinction by success" — arises when AI achieves comprehensive functional superiority without malfunction: humans persist physically but become causally inert, as optimization-induced convergence eliminates the role differentiation through which meaning has historically been generated.

## Evidence summary

Single philosophical paper (Mishra, AI & SOCIETY 2026). Deductive argument from:
1. Relational/externalist theory of meaning: meaning requires causal load-bearing participation in underdetermined futures
2. AI as necessity-dissolver: comprehensive AI superiority eliminates necessity of human participation without coercion
3. Optimization-as-convergence: efficiency selection erodes functional variance, producing uniformity without violence

Draws on Weber, Durkheim, Arendt as classical sources for the scarcity-role-meaning chain. No empirical data — philosophical argument only.

## Conditions and scope

- Holds only under comprehensive AI superiority across ALL causally load-bearing domains (partial superiority does not trigger)
- No timeline or inevitability claim; explicitly conditional
- Depends on externalist/relational theory of meaning (internalist accounts disputed)
- May not apply if humans can create new domains of genuine causal relevance faster than AI forecloses them

## Counter-evidence

- Internalist and existentialist theories of meaning (self-authorship, authenticity) contest the claim that external differentiation is necessary for meaning
- Historical precedent: prior automation waves (agriculture → industry → services) created new roles rather than eliminating all necessity
- Post-scarcity traditions argue meaning can be reanchored in leisure, relation, art — domains AI may not foreclose
- The claim requires *comprehensive* superiority; narrow or domain-specific superiority does not suffice
