## Statement

AI capabilities (measured by benchmark performance, model scale, and application diversity) and AI investment (compute, R&D spending, venture capital) have advanced at historically unprecedented rates each year from 2022 through 2025, with generative AI achieving enterprise-scale adoption faster than any prior technology wave.

## Evidence summary

Convergent evidence from multiple independent annual reports: State of AI (Air Street Capital), HAI AI Index (Stanford), McKinsey surveys, BOND/Meeker analysis. High confidence because consistent across multiple independent data sources and methodologies.

## Conditions and scope

- Applies to frontier model capabilities and aggregate investment
- Sectoral and geographic variation exists; some industries and countries lag significantly
- Rate of advance may decelerate as hardware bottlenecks and diminishing returns set in

## Counter-evidence

- Productivity statistics have not yet reflected AI investment (see economists-expect-ai-capability-gains-without)
- Inference efficiency improvements (DeepSeek) may reduce total compute investment despite capability growth
