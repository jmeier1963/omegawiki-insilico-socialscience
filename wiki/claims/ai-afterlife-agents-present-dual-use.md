## Statement

AI agents modeled on deceased individuals (generative ghosts) can provide genuine value — grief support, knowledge preservation, memorialization — while simultaneously posing serious risks — impeding grief, violating consent, enabling manipulation and misinformation — requiring structured governance and research before widespread deployment.

## Evidence summary

Morris and Brubaker (2024) provide design space analysis identifying four implementation dimensions and map potential benefits (grief support, expert knowledge preservation) and risks (psychological harm, consent/dignity violations, misuse). No empirical study yet — design and ethics contribution only.

## Conditions and scope

Speculative/prospective analysis. No longitudinal data on grief outcomes. Risk/benefit balance is highly context-dependent (consent presence, fidelity level, supervision).

## Counter-evidence

No empirical studies available yet (2024 paper). Potential benefits remain theoretical; risks also untested at scale.

## Open questions

- Can opt-in consent systems adequately protect against harmful posthumous uses?
