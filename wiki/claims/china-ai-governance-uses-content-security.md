## Statement

China's generative AI governance framework uses technically demanding content security standards — covering training data, output filtering, and alignment mechanisms — primarily to ensure AI outputs align with "socialist core values" and avoid politically sensitive content, rather than for safety in the Western sense.

## Evidence summary

Technical analysis by ChinaTalk (2025) based on the 2023 Interim Measures and subsequent standards. Confidence moderate: based on good-faith analysis of primary Chinese regulatory documents; technical implementation details may not be fully accurate.

## Counter-evidence

- Chinese AI safety requirements also include genuine safety provisions (biosecurity, cybersecurity) beyond political content control
- Some compliance requirements overlap with Western AI safety best practices
