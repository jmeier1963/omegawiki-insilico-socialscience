## Statement

The freshwater footprint of AI (cooling + electricity generation + manufacturing) is large in absolute terms, varies sharply across location and time, and is currently under-measured and under-disclosed relative to AI's carbon footprint.

## Evidence summary

A single principled, scope-based methodology applied to GPT-3 and to 2027 global projections yields multi-billion-m³ withdrawal estimates and ~9× spatial variation in cooling efficiency; water usage is routinely absent from model cards while carbon is reported.

## Conditions and scope

Holds under current data-center cooling and grid assumptions; estimates are conservative but uncertain. Carbon and water can trade off, so the claim is about *measurement and disclosure gaps* as much as magnitude.

## Counter-evidence

None recorded in-wiki. Market-based accounting (renewable/offset programs) can lower reported footprints relative to location-based estimates.

## Open questions

Standardized water disclosure; joint carbon–water optimization; equitable siting under freshwater stress.
