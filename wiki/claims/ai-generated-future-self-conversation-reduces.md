## Statement

A single brief session interacting with a personalized AI-generated version of one's future self — the "Future You" system — significantly reduces self-reported anxiety and negative emotions, and increases future self-continuity, compared to control conditions (passive reflection or generic AI chat without personalization).

## Evidence summary

Pataranutaporn et al. (2024) conducted a 4-condition RCT (n=344): Future You, Questionnaire, generic Chat, and Control. The Future You condition produced the largest anxiety reductions (mean Δ=-0.68 vs. Control Δ=+0.21, p=0.001) and significant improvements in future self-continuity. The generic Chat condition showed some but smaller effects, suggesting personalization is important. The study was preregistered and MIT IRB approved.

## Conditions and scope

- Applies to single-session interventions with healthy adult participants
- Requires a personalized AI system (generic chatbot shows attenuated effects)
- Population: likely tech-literate, English-speaking, self-selected
- Long-term persistence not established

## Counter-evidence

- Single study; no independent replications as of 2025
- Small per-condition sample (n=73 in Future You arm)
- Effects may be driven by novelty/expectations rather than the intervention mechanism
- Long-term wellbeing effects and potential negative outcomes (attachment, unrealistic expectations) not measured

## Open questions

- Do effects replicate in more diverse populations?
- What is the contribution of the age-progressed photo vs. the personalized LLM conversation?
- Can the intervention produce lasting changes in future self-continuity, or are effects short-lived?
