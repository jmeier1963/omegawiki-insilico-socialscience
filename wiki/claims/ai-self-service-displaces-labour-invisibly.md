## Statement

AI's principal labour-market effect is self-service displacement — enabling customers to do work themselves and collapsing demand for intermediary services — rather than machines doing jobs in place. Because consumer-absorbed work leaves the measured economy, this both displaces labour and creates a growing GDP/labour-statistics blind spot, reversing capitalism's historical move of activity from household into market.

## Evidence summary

A chain of historical cases (laundress/washing machine, self-checkout, online travel and brokerages) plus AI extensions to trades and professions; the verification-as-binding-constraint point (Catalini) explains why self-service offloads the residual burden to customers.

## Conditions and scope

Self-serviceable intermediary services; 2026 framing; unquantified relative magnitude.

## Counter-evidence

The Jevons paradox (in-service automation lowering price and *growing* employment) and the [[ai-normal-technology]] view bound how much self-service displacement actually dominates; some services resist self-service.
