## Statement

On its current trajectory Europe will be economically and politically sidelined by AI; the decisive lever is sovereign AI compute — building tens of gigawatts on European soil under EU jurisdiction (via fast-tracked Special Compute Zones), partnering with US hyperscalers while "bolting" datacentres to European law, and coordinating a coalition of middle powers — paired with flexicurity-style labour reform and a positive political vision.

## Evidence summary

The Europe 2031 scenario traces a plausible path from 2025 to a 2031 Washington room where "Europe walks in with nothing left to trade," and its epilogue enumerates the 2026 counterfactual measures (compute, middle-power coalition, robotics partnerships, flexicurity, narrative) that could have changed course.

## Conditions and scope

EU / European middle powers; 2026 decision window; contingent on AI pace assumptions.

## Counter-evidence

The [[ai-normal-technology]] view would temper the urgency (capability ≠ automatic transformation); critics may see compute-sovereignty as infeasible given the ~5% starting share and 27-state coordination problems the report itself dramatizes.
