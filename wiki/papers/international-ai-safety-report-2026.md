## Problem

What are the capabilities, risks, and risk mitigation options for frontier AI systems? Policymakers need a rigorous, internationally-endorsed evidence base to inform consequential decisions about general-purpose AI.

## Key idea

**Second international consensus assessment of frontier AI safety**: synthesizes evidence from 100+ experts across 30+ countries on AI capabilities, emerging risks (risks arising at the capability frontier), and risk management techniques. Chaired by Yoshua Bengio; builds on the 2023 Bletchley Park mandate.

## Method

- Synthesis report; not original research
- Contributions from 100+ experts, guided by experts nominated by 30+ countries and intergovernmental organisations
- Scope narrowed from v1 to "emerging risks" — frontier capability risks specifically
- Incorporates OECD + Forecasting Research Institute scenario forecasts
- Reviewed by civil society, industry, and government advisers

## Results

- Rigorous consensus assessment of: current frontier AI capabilities; emerging risks from capability advances; available risk mitigation techniques
- Forecasting scenarios from OECD and FRI on AI progress trajectories
- Emphasis on international coordination as a key governance requirement

## Limitations

- Synthesis report; positions may represent consensus rather than frontier scientific views
- Scope limited to "emerging risks" from frontier AI; does not cover existing AI harms comprehensively
- Some senior advisers' views may not be fully reflected in consensus positions

## Open questions

- How should international governance frameworks be structured to respond to AI safety findings?
- How does the 2026 report's risk assessment compare to the 2023 baseline?

## My take

The second international AI safety report represents a significant governance milestone — a 100+ expert consensus document endorsed by 30+ countries. For understanding the international policy landscape of AI safety, this is a primary reference. Importance 4 — key policy document with direct governance relevance.
