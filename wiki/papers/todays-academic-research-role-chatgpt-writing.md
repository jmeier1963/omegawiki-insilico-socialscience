## Problem

ChatGPT's rapid adoption in academic writing raises questions about benefits, risks, and integrity implications.

## Key idea

Rapid literature review synthesizing ChatGPT's roles in academic research: writing support, data analysis, literature review generation, and scientific collaboration. Includes discussion of plagiarism risks and responsible use.

## Method

Rapid literature review (narrative). South Africa-based author.

## Results

ChatGPT can assist with: drafting, paraphrasing, literature search, data summaries, and collaborative writing. Risk of plagiarism if outputs are not properly attributed; responsible use requires transparency and critical oversight.

## Limitations

Rapid narrative review, no systematic methodology, rapidly outdated (2024 coverage), lower-tier venue.

## Open questions

- How can institutions operationalize "responsible use" guidelines that are enforceable?

Descriptive overview, minimal research contribution. Useful only as practitioner-facing synthesis.
