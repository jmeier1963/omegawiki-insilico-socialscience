## Problem

The UK needs a national strategy to capture AI's economic opportunities while building domestic AI capabilities and reducing dependence on foreign AI infrastructure.

## Key idea

UK government AI Opportunities Action Plan (published 13 January 2025) organized around three pillars: (1) laying foundations (compute, data, skills infrastructure); (2) changing lives through AI adoption in public services; (3) securing the future with homegrown AI development.

## Method

Government policy document. Independent report presented to Parliament. Outlines specific commitments on AI compute investment, public sector AI adoption, and support for UK AI companies.

## Results

- Three-pillar strategy: infrastructure foundations, AI adoption, homegrown AI capability
- Emphasis on sovereign compute: UK needs domestic AI compute capacity
- Public sector as early adopter and testbed for AI applications
- Support for AI talent, startups, and research ecosystem
- Benchmarks set against international competitors (US, EU)

## Limitations

- Government policy documents reflect political priorities; implementation uncertain
- "Homegrown AI" ambitions may be unrealistic given resource gap vs US/China
- No binding enforcement mechanisms; depends on political will

## Open questions

- How will the UK balance AI adoption with safety regulation?
- Can UK AI compute investments attract sufficient private co-investment?

## My take

Standard government AI strategy document. Useful for tracking UK AI policy evolution. The three-pillar structure is coherent. Importance 3 (official government strategy).
