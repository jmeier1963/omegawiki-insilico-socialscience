## Problem

How did Microsoft's partnership with OpenAI develop, what was their strategy for responsible AI deployment, and how did the OpenAI board crisis (Altman ouster) affect it?

## Key idea

Charles Duhigg's New Yorker longform (December 2023) on the Microsoft-OpenAI partnership, focusing on Microsoft CTO Kevin Scott and the development of a "protocol for releasing AI ambitiously but safely." The article covers: how Microsoft came to bet billions on OpenAI, their joint responsible AI framework, and how the OpenAI board's sudden ouster of Sam Altman (November 2023) threatened but ultimately strengthened the partnership.

## Method

Longform journalism. 19 pages. Charles Duhigg (New Yorker). Based on interviews with Kevin Scott, Satya Nadella, and others.

## Results

- Microsoft's AI bet: Kevin Scott convinced Satya Nadella that GPT was transformative; led to multibillion-dollar OpenAI investment
- Responsible AI protocol: Microsoft and OpenAI developed joint framework for releasing AI "ambitiously but safely"
- November 2023 Altman ouster: shocked Microsoft, who had no advance notice; Nadella immediately began contingency planning
- Outcome: Altman reinstated; Microsoft came out stronger with board observer seat
- Kevin Scott's philosophy: "You have to experiment in public" — accept public failures as part of responsible AI deployment

## Limitations

- Journalism; based on interviews, subject to access bias
- Microsoft's perspective dominates; OpenAI board's perspective less represented

## My take

Important account of the formative Microsoft-OpenAI relationship. The "experiment in public" philosophy and the responsible AI protocol are analytically useful for understanding how frontier AI is being deployed. Importance 2 (journalism; valuable historical account).
