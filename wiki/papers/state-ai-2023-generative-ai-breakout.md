## Problem

How quickly is generative AI being adopted across industries? What effects do organizations expect on their industries and workforces?

## Key idea

McKinsey Global Survey on AI (August 2023, 24 pages). Documents the rapid adoption of generative AI tools within a year of their debut: one-third of respondents report their organizations already using gen AI in at least one business function.

## Method

McKinsey annual AI survey. Quantitative survey of enterprise decision-makers and employees across industries and geographies.

## Results

- One-third of survey respondents: organizations using gen AI in at least one business function
- Significant expected effects on industry and workforce
- AI adoption outpacing previous technology waves in speed of corporate uptake
- Productivity gains reported but difficult to measure systematically

## My take

Important data point on the speed of GenAI enterprise adoption. The 1/3 adoption rate in less than a year is remarkable and well-documented. Importance 3 (survey evidence with good methodology).
