## Problem

How large is the AI education opportunity and what are the key adoption metrics?

## Key idea

Bond Capital's investor-facing report synthesizing AI education adoption data, ChatGPT momentum metrics (users, usage, monetization), and market opportunity analysis. July 1, 2024.

## Method

Market analysis from VC investor perspective; aggregated from public data, industry reports, and proprietary sources.

## Results

ChatGPT adoption in education markets described as extraordinary business/societal inflection; education sector adoption metrics, monetization trends, and competitive dynamics summarized.

## Limitations

VC investor framing optimizes for market opportunity narrative; not independent academic analysis.

## My take

Useful for tracking adoption velocity and investor enthusiasm, less useful for understanding educational outcomes.
