## Problem

70% of foundational AI models are US-developed; China is rapidly expanding its share. Europe risks structural dependence on foreign AI infrastructure, creating vulnerabilities in data security, economic competitiveness, and democratic values alignment.

## Key idea

Proposes EuroStack: a comprehensive European technology stack covering AI platforms, cloud infrastructure, and federated data spaces. Aims to reduce foreign dependencies and establish Europe as a leader in "public interest AI" aligned with European values of transparency, accountability, and privacy.

## Method

Policy analysis and strategic framework. Commissioned report led by Francesca Bria (formerly Barcelona Chief Digital Officer). Outlines investment requirements, governance structures, and technical architecture for European AI sovereignty.

## Results

- AI identified as cornerstone of EuroStack: powers data-driven decision making across manufacturing, healthcare, public services, energy
- Sovereign AI platforms + federated data spaces as core infrastructure components
- Requires coordinated industrial policy, public investment, and regulatory alignment across EU member states
- Analogous to prior European industrial policy successes (Airbus model)

## Limitations

- Resource requirements are very large; political coordination across EU member states is challenging
- "Sovereign AI" may lag US/China frontier models significantly
- Risk of creating European tech protectionism that stifles innovation

## Open questions

- Can European sovereign AI platforms attract top global AI talent?
- How does EuroStack interact with existing EU AI Act governance?

## My take

Important policy framework for European AI governance debates. The EuroStack vision is ambitious but faces real coordination and competitiveness challenges. Important reading for EU AI policy. Importance 3 (influential policy report, not empirical research).
