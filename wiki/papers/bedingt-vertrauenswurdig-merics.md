## Problem

How close are Huawei's ties to the Chinese Communist Party, and what does this mean for the security of 5G and digital infrastructure?

## Key idea

Investigative journalism (Handelsblatt) examining Huawei's connections to the Chinese Communist Party (KPCh), based on visits to Huawei's Shenzhen headquarters and the Songshan Lake Campus. Explores the question of whether European countries can trust Huawei for critical digital infrastructure. The "conditionally trustworthy" verdict: Huawei is commercially sophisticated but security concerns from CCP ties are real and difficult to dismiss.

## Method

Investigative journalism. 11 pages (print newspaper format). Multiple reporters (Rexer, Kort, Meiritz, Fischer, Leitel, Heide, Koch, Jahn).

## Results

- Huawei Shenzhen HQ: luxury facilities, impressive scale, commercially sophisticated
- CCP ties: complex; Huawei has party committee structures, but operational independence unclear
- Security risk assessment: European governments increasingly excluding Huawei from core 5G networks
- The "trust" question cannot be answered definitively; structural CCP-corporate ties create systemic risk
