## Problem

How are global CEOs responding to unprecedented complexity and uncertainty, including the AI transition?

## Key idea

Personal report from a CEO survey (1,235 global CEOs). Key finding: 92% of CEOs agree they must cultivate adaptability "beyond anything previously imagined." While 72% anticipate stagnating global prosperity, nearly all see opportunities through AI, local anchoring, and resilience building. Decisive shift: adaptability and agility now essential; scripted approaches abandoned.

## Method

Survey of 1,235 global CEOs. Personalized report format. Published 2025 (labeled "Strictly confidential" — likely executive survey firm report). 16 pages.

## Results

- 92% say adaptability is now more important than ever
- 72% anticipate stagnating global prosperity
- Nearly all CEOs see AI as key opportunity amid uncertainty
- Shift: from scripted playbooks to improvisation-based leadership
- Focus: localizing supply chains, leveraging AI, building resilience

## Limitations

- Survey methodology not detailed in extracted text
- "Confidential" personal report — not designed for public reference
- Selection bias: CEOs who participate in such surveys may differ from general CEO population

## My take

Business intelligence document useful as evidence of how C-suite leadership perceives AI adoption and uncertainty in 2025. The 92% adaptability figure is striking. Importance 2 (survey data; useful for enterprise AI adoption context).
