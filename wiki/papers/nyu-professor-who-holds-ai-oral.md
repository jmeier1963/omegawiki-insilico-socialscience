## Problem

Students' AI-generated written work sounds like McKinsey memos; distinguishing authentic student work from AI output is increasingly difficult.

## Key idea

A business school professor's response to AI-generated writing: switch to oral examinations ("fight fire with fire") to test genuine understanding where AI cannot assist in real-time.

## Method

Journalistic case study / practitioner account.

## Results

Oral exams successfully exposed the gap between polished AI-generated written submissions and students' actual understanding. Practical workaround for assessment integrity.

## Limitations

Anecdotal, single professor, specific business school context.

## My take

A concrete example of the oral examination solution proposed by Bieger & Kolmar and Clay Shirky's NYT essay. The McKinsey memo framing is memorable.
