## Problem

Comprehensive annual assessment of AI progress across technical, economic, policy, and societal dimensions.

## Key idea

Eighth edition of the Stanford HAI AI Index Report (2025). 456 pages. New in 2025: in-depth AI hardware landscape, novel inference cost estimates, AI publication and patenting trends. Most comprehensive AI tracking report available.

## Method

Original data collection and analysis across: technical performance benchmarks, investment data, patent analysis, policy tracking, education data, public opinion surveys. Authoritative reference compiled by Stanford HAI research team.

## Results

Full content not extracted (456-page reference document). 2025 edition adds hardware landscape and inference cost analysis — reflecting shift to inference-time compute as key economic driver. Covers all major AI development dimensions with original data.

## Limitations

- Comprehensive scope means depth per topic is limited
- Funded by university and industry partners; balanced but not independent

## My take

The definitive annual AI statistics reference. The 2025 inference cost analysis is particularly important given the shift toward inference-time compute. Importance 4 (essential annual reference for AI field).
