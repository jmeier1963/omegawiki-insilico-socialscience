## Problem

Higher education's core purpose — developing independent thinking, genuine engagement with ideas, intellectual integrity — is threatened by the convergence of grade inflation and AI.

## Key idea

Frank Bruni's opinion essay connecting two converging crises: long-running grade inflation (lowered standards, reduced intellectual rigor) and AI's ability to further decouple performance from genuine understanding.

## Method

Opinion essay.

## Results

Argues that the two trends (grade inflation + AI) are mutually reinforcing: inflated expectations create pressure to use AI, which further inflates grades while hollowing out learning.

## Limitations

Opinion, no data.

## My take

Makes the connection between pre-existing academic culture problems and AI adoption explicit. Grade inflation as a structural enabler of AI misuse is an underappreciated angle.
