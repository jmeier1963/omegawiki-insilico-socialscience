## Problem

Europe needs a long-term strategic framework for digital sovereignty, analogous to the Rockefeller Special Studies Project that helped the US navigate the atomic age in 1956.

## Key idea

Funding proposal by Prof. Francesca Bria (Barcelona, formerly) for a 3-year Digital Sovereignty Special Studies Project. Builds on EuroStack framework to develop a comprehensive European technology strategy for 2030.

## Method

Research/policy proposal document. Outlines research agenda, methodology, and deliverables. €1.5 million over 3 years.

## Results

- Proposal for a multi-disciplinary research hub modeled on Rockefeller Special Studies Project
- Combines industry, academia, policy, and media perspectives on European digital strategy
- Core focus: EuroStack infrastructure, digital sovereignty governance, AI strategy
- Argues current ad-hoc European responses are insufficient for the scale of challenge

## Limitations

- Funding proposal; describes intended work rather than completed research
- May not reflect final project scope (if funded)
