## Problem

The scientific study of catastrophic risks from AI has fallen far short of what's needed. As AI systems approach AGI, a structured, science-based framework for tracking, evaluating, forecasting, and protecting against catastrophic risks is required.

## Key idea

**Five-element preparedness framework**: (1) Tracking catastrophic risk level via evaluations and scorecards; (2) Seeking unknown-unknowns; (3) Establishing safety baselines (only models with post-mitigation risk ≤ "medium" can be deployed); (4) Protecting against emergent risks during deployment; (5) Maintaining a safety buffer.

## Method

- Institutional framework document
- Tracked Risk Categories (specific catastrophic risk areas to monitor)
- Scorecard: pre-mitigation and post-mitigation risk levels per category
- Safety baseline: post-mitigation risk must be ≤ "medium" for deployment approval
- Forecasting of future risk development for lead time on safety measures

## Results

- Framework document; not empirical
- Establishes the institutional vocabulary for AI preparedness at OpenAI
- Beta version: "living document" to be updated as AI capabilities develop

## Limitations

- Internal company document; not independently verified
- Risk categories and thresholds are defined by OpenAI
- "Medium risk" threshold for deployment is subjective

## My take

A foundational governance document establishing how a leading AI lab conceptualizes catastrophic risk management. Important for understanding the institutional AI safety discourse as of late 2023. Importance 3 — practitioner/policy document, influential for AI governance frameworks.
