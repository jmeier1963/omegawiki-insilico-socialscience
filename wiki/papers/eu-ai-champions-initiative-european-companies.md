## Problem

How can European companies coordinate to build competitive AI capabilities and capture the economic opportunity of AI?

## Key idea

Media release for the EU AI Champions Initiative, launched at the AI Action Summit in Paris (2025). 60+ leading European companies (Airbus, ASML, Axa, BNP Paribas, Deutsche Bank, Lufthansa, Mercedes-Benz, Mistral AI, SAP, Siemens, Spotify, Volkswagen Group, and others) forming a coalition to accelerate Europe's AI momentum. Initiative aims to: coordinate AI adoption across technology, industry, capital, and policy; mobilize talent and capital; accelerate AI adoption in established industries; shape competitive European technology companies.

## Method

Industry press release. 5 pages. Coalition announcement document.

## Results

- Coalition: 60+ European companies across sectors
- Announced at Paris AI Action Summit by French President Macron
- Three strategic pillars: mobilize talent/capital, accelerate AI adoption in incumbents, shape European AI companies
- Explicitly frames mission as helping Europe compete with US and China

## Limitations

- Industry press release; no analytical depth
- Coalition interests vary; coordination may be limited in practice

## My take

Significant industry signal of European corporate AI ambition. The coalition breadth (aerospace, finance, automotive, tech) is noteworthy. Importance 2 (industry press release; useful as evidence of European corporate AI coordination effort).
