## Problem

Public debates about regulation focus on government red tape, but a parallel form of private "red tape" — the compliance costs, barriers, and dependencies imposed by dominant platform gatekeepers — is larger and largely invisible in policy debates.

## Key idea

Argues that concentrated private market power (dominant tech platforms as gatekeepers) functions analogously to excessive government regulation: creating barriers to entry, imposing compliance costs on dependent businesses, and stifling competition and innovation. Coined "the other red tape" to reframe the deregulation debate.

## Method

Policy analysis and case studies by Denise Hearn, Nidhi Hegde, and Matt Stoller (American Economic Liberties Project, June 2021). Reviews examples of gatekeeper power across tech platforms, financial services, and other concentrated industries.

## Results

- Private gatekeeper "red tape" includes: app store commissions, API access controls, algorithmic ranking power, terms of service enforcement
- These private costs are often larger than government compliance costs for small businesses
- Market concentration in tech has accelerated since 2008; barriers to entry are now structural not just behavioral
- Policy implication: antitrust and structural remedies (interoperability, divestiture) are necessary complements to deregulation

## Limitations

- Advocacy document from anti-monopoly organization; selection bias in examples
- Does not quantify private red tape costs empirically
- Written before major antitrust enforcement actions (2022–2025)

## Open questions

- Has antitrust enforcement (Google, Apple, Meta cases) materially reduced gatekeeper power?
- Does the "other red tape" concept apply to AI platforms and frontier model providers?

## My take

Useful conceptual reframe for platform power debates. The parallel to government red tape is rhetorically effective and analytically interesting. The concentration→gatekeeper→barrier logic applies directly to AI model providers and cloud platforms. Importance 3 (influential advocacy document).
