## Problem

Young people are using AI to handle difficult social and emotional situations (conflict resolution, difficult conversations, emotional processing) that are essential for psychological development.

## Key idea

NYT opinion piece (January 2026) arguing that young people using AI to bypass the hardest interpersonal and emotional experiences of adolescence risk missing critical developmental experiences that build social competence, resilience, and emotional intelligence.

## Method

Opinion essay.

## Results

Normative warning: the developmental value of struggle is being circumvented by AI. Extends the cognitive debt argument to social-emotional development.

## Limitations

Opinion, no empirical data on developmental outcomes.

## Open questions

- Is there evidence that AI-mediated social interaction substitution harms long-term social development?

## My take

Extends the cognitive/epistemic off-loading concern to social-emotional domains. Relevant to future self-continuity research (FutureYou) and human flourishing frameworks.
