## Problem

What are the key trends and changes to watch in 2022 across culture, technology, and society?

## Key idea

Annual consumer and cultural trends report by Wunderman Thompson Intelligence (advertising/marketing firm). 223 pages. "The Future 100" covers 100 trends across culture, beauty, fashion, food, travel, innovation, health, and technology.

## Method

Wunderman Thompson Intelligence trend research. Qualitative analysis of emerging cultural and technological trends. By Emma Chiu (Global Director, Wunderman Thompson Intelligence).

## Results

Full content not extracted (223-page reference document). Marketing/consumer trends perspective on AI and technology developments in 2022 context.

## Limitations

- Marketing firm perspective; commercial framing
- Pre-ChatGPT era; many AI trend assessments now historical

## My take

Consumer trends reference document. Less rigorous than academic or policy sources. Useful for cultural context of AI adoption. Importance 2 (commercial trend report).
