## Problem

Comprehensive data-driven overview of AI trends across capabilities, investment, adoption, compute, and competition dynamics.

## Key idea

Mary Meeker's first dedicated AI Trends report (May 2025, BOND Capital). 340 pages. Co-authored by Jay Simons, Daegwon Chae, Alexander Krey. Applies Meeker's signature data-dense slide format (from the famous Internet Trends reports) to AI. Covers foundational trends: compute scaling, data scaling, model capabilities, investment, adoption, international competition, and AI product emergence.

## Method

Data compilation and analysis. Dense slide format with charts and data across the AI ecosystem. "A collection of several disparate datapoints" on foundational AI trends — self-described as a comprehensive foundational reference.

## Results

Full content not extracted (340-page slide deck reference). Expected key themes from context: compute-capability relationships, investment acceleration, US-China competition dynamics, AI product market emergence, Jevons Paradox in AI infrastructure, scaling law trajectories. Document explicitly frames accelerating competition between tech incumbents, emerging attackers, and sovereigns.

## Limitations

- VC perspective (BOND Capital); may have investment thesis biases
- Slide format limits narrative depth per topic
- Published May 2025; fast-moving field

## My take

One of the most important AI trend analyses of 2025. Meeker's Internet Trends reports were defining documents for the internet era; this marks her applying the same framework to AI. The framing of competition among incumbents, attackers, and sovereigns is analytically useful. Importance 4 (highly influential annual reference from credible analyst).
