## Problem

China's GenAI Content Security Standard governs what AI systems deployed in China must and cannot generate. The regulatory requirements are technical and specific — what do they actually mean for AI developers?

## Key idea

Technical + regulatory explainer of China's generative AI governance framework. Covers the 2023 Interim Measures for Generative AI Services and subsequent content security standards. Explains what Chinese AI engineers must actually implement to get regulatory approval.

## Method

Technical analysis by "Bit Wise" (ChinaTalk newsletter contributor). Combines technical AI knowledge with Chinese regulatory framework analysis. Published January 2025.

## Results

- China's Interim Measures (July 2023) require genAI providers to: (1) perform algorithm registration, (2) implement content security measures, (3) ensure AI outputs align with "socialist core values"
- Content security standard specifies prohibited content categories: politically sensitive topics, pornography, violence, "spreading rumors"
- Technical implementation requires: training data filtering, output filtering, RLHF alignment toward state-approved values
- Described as "SB 1047 with Socialist Characteristics" — technically demanding requirements with political rather than safety motivations
- Algorithm registration reveals model architecture and training data to regulators

## Limitations

- Newsletter analysis; may miss technical or legal nuances
- Chinese regulatory context evolves rapidly; may be partially outdated
