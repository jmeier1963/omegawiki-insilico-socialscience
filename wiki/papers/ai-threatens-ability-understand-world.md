## Problem

Students using AI to generate answers without engaging in the cognitive work of understanding risk developing a surface relationship with knowledge.

## Key idea

NYT opinion piece arguing that AI tools, when used to bypass the effortful process of working through problems, threaten students' capacity to genuinely understand the world — not just recall or produce answers. The argument extends the "cognitive debt" concern to epistemic capacities.

## Method

Opinion essay. Published October 29, 2025.

## Results

Normative argument: AI-mediated shortcuts in learning threaten the development of genuine understanding (comprehension, causal reasoning, worldview integration), not just rote skills.

## Limitations

Opinion piece without empirical data.

## Open questions

- Is there empirical evidence that AI-assisted learning reduces depth of understanding vs. breadth of exposure?

## My take

Consistent with the cognitive debt literature (Kosmyna et al.) and the human flourishing framework. Strong rhetorical piece for public discourse.
