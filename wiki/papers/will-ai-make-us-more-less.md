## Problem

Does AI assistance make humans wiser (by providing better information and analysis) or less wise (by atrophying judgment, reducing lived experience of difficulty)?

## Key idea

Substack essay by Michael Spencer and Chad Woodford exploring whether AI enhances or diminishes human wisdom — distinguishing knowledge (information access) from wisdom (judgment developed through experience and reflection).

## Method

Philosophical essay, newsletter format. February 27, 2025.

## Results

Argues that AI optimizes for knowledge transfer but not wisdom development; wisdom requires the friction of experience, failure, and reflection that AI shortcuts.

## Limitations

Newsletter format, not peer-reviewed. Speculative.

## My take

The knowledge/wisdom distinction is important and underused in AI-education debates. Links to the human flourishing framework's emphasis on "acting in the world" and ethical competence as experientially developed.
