## Problem

Public discourse on AI in higher education often focuses on risks (cheating, cognitive off-loading). What does a thoughtful professor's actual classroom experience look like?

## Key idea

NYT Magazine personal essay by a professor describing how AI integration has transformed their teaching — not negatively but in ways that require adaptation and create new opportunities for deeper engagement. Published November 25, 2025.

## Method

First-person narrative essay with classroom observation.

## Results

The author finds AI can free students from lower-order cognitive tasks and create space for higher-order discussion when thoughtfully integrated. The fear-vs-reality gap for faculty is significant.

## Limitations

Personal narrative, single instructor, not generalizable.

## Open questions

- What distinguishes faculty who experience AI integration positively vs. negatively?

## My take

A useful counterweight to panic-driven AI-in-education discourse. Consistent with Mollick's practitioner optimism.
