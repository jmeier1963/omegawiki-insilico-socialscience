## Problem

Sub-Saharan African students often lack access to high-quality educational resources; AI chatbots represent a potential leapfrog technology for educational access.

## Key idea

World Bank blog post describing an AI after-school program in Edo State, Nigeria, where students learn through conversational AI chatbots. Frames AI as an equalizing educational technology for the developing world.

## Method

Journalistic description of a pilot program.

## Results

Positive early outcomes reported from the Edo State program; AI chatbots provide personalized learning at low cost relative to human tutors.

## Limitations

Blog post, no controlled evaluation. Pilot-scale only.

## My take

The positive equity narrative (AI as leapfrog for the Global South) directly complements and complicates the German AI disadvantaged communities analysis (where AI is risk-amplifying). Context and implementation quality are decisive.
