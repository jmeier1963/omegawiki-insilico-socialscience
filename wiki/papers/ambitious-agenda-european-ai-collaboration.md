## Problem

Europe lags the US and China in AI capability development and adoption. What specific actions are needed across key sectors to accelerate European AI?

## Key idea

Industry report arguing for "radical collaboration" approach to European AI adoption. Covers AI use cases and adoption barriers across nine sectors: Aerospace & Defence, Automotive, Consumer Retail, Energy, Enterprise Software, Financial Services, Life Sciences & Healthcare, Robotics, Shipping & Logistics, Telecommunications.

## Method

Industry report by unspecified authors (no individual authors named). Sector spotlights + policy recommendations. Published February 2025.

## Results

- "AI imperative for Europe": without acceleration, European industry loses competitiveness
- "Radical collaboration" between industry, academia, and government required
- Sector-specific barriers: data access, regulation, talent, infrastructure
- Policy recommendations focus on procurement, compute investment, and regulatory fast-tracks

## Limitations

- Industry-commissioned report; likely reflects industry interests
- No authors identified; attribution unclear
- Sector spotlights are brief, not empirical analyses

## Open questions

- Which European sectors show actual AI adoption acceleration?

## My take

Standard industry advocacy report. Useful for sector context. Less rigorous than academic or parliamentary sources. Importance 2 (industry report).
