## Problem

AI hallucinations create a specific pedagogical problem: students submit confidently stated false information that they cannot detect as false without domain expertise.

## Key idea

FAZ article by Markus Steinmayr (December 31, 2025) documenting how AI hallucinations have changed German university teaching. Faculty describe increased burden of detecting plausible-sounding but false AI-generated content in student work.

## Method

Newspaper journalism with faculty interviews.

## Results

Faculty report significant increases in plausible-sounding but factually incorrect student submissions; hallucination detection requires expert knowledge that students don't yet have; creates a "confidence without competence" problem.

## Limitations

Journalistic, no systematic data.

## My take

The hallucination problem in education creates a qualitatively different integrity challenge than simple copying: the student may not know they submitted false information. This compounds the cognitive debt argument.
