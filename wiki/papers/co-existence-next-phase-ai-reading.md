## Problem & Context

Source is a personal Kindle highlights export (71 highlights, 1 bookmark, dated 22 Oct 2026), not the full text, so the page captures only what the reader marked. The book asks how people and organizations should live with AI whose capabilities may approach those of top experts.

## Key idea

Highlighted claims: AI capability is *jagged* (shaped by scale, data diversity, training decisions and the structure of human knowledge), so no one can precisely map where it is strong; this jaggedness yields many configurations where human and AI strengths complement each other; and a *Wait Calculation* applies when technology improves fast enough that waiting beats acting now.

## Method

Argument by example and analogy rather than new experiments. Highlighted example: BloombergGPT (about 2e23 FLOPs, domain data) was outperformed by general-purpose GPT-4 (about 2e25 FLOPs), illustrating that scale can beat specialized data.

## Experiment & Results

No experiments. Notable highlighted lines: AI is 'genuinely powerful and genuinely unreliable'; the goal is judgment about which regime one faces; durable human value includes 'taste, deep expertise, broad contextual knowledge, and the agency to act on uncertain information'.
