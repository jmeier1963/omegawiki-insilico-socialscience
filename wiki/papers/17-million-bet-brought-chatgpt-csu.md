## Problem

After a $17M institutional bet on ChatGPT across the California State University system, mixed results and significant distrust among faculty and students emerge one year later.

## Key idea

LA Times investigation into CSU's ChatGPT adoption: faculty resistance, student skepticism, lack of AI literacy training, and demand for transparency about how AI is being used in evaluation.

## Method

Investigative journalism. April 2026.

## Results

One year in: AI adoption uneven across campuses, significant distrust, lack of training, and students demanding clearer guidance on acceptable AI use.

## Limitations

Journalistic snapshot, not systematic evaluation.

## My take

A follow-up data point to the original NYT Cal State piece; reinforces that top-down institutional AI mandates without bottom-up support create distrust.
